# UK courts in Find Case Law: subjects and existing taxonomies

**Checked:** 4 November 2026. This page maps the court and court-division entries on The National Archives' [Find Case Law courts-and-coverage page](https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/courts-and-coverage) to a brief description of their work. It checks the investigation's [taxonomy index](../index.md) for an existing extraction and, where none is linked, points to an official GOV.UK or judiciary description that could seed a subject list.

The Find Case Law date ranges below are the published bounds shown by the service, not a guarantee of complete coverage. A GOV.UK or judiciary “What we do” description is a **source for candidate subject headings**, not necessarily a controlled taxonomy used to tag judgments.

### Reading the court list

- The directory preserves Find Case Law's display labels and series identifiers, including historical or reporting-only labels. Its coverage dates are publication bounds, not a statement that every decision within that jurisdiction is online.
- High Court labels reflect both current and older structures. The judiciary's current description uses **Business and Property Division**; Find Case Law also retains a **Chancery Division** series. The **King’s / Queen’s Bench Division** is a combined historical service label, while the current division is King's Bench.
- **Mercantile Court** is a historical label; the GOV.UK Business and Property Courts page calls its successor the Circuit Commercial Court.
- The **Family Court (High Court judges)** entry is not the same thing as the High Court's Family Division.
- The High Court **Technology and Construction Court** is distinct from the **Upper Tribunal (Tax and Chancery Chamber)** (UT(TCC)); only the latter has the [six-category GOV.UK finder taxonomy](../taxonomy-extractions/tax-and-chancery-tribunal-taxonomy.md).

## Sources and method

- Starting list, displayed names and date ranges: [Find Case Law — Courts and coverage](https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/courts-and-coverage), checked 4 November 2026. Each court link in the table opens the corresponding Find Case Law search route.
- Taxonomy status: checked against this investigation's [index of extractions](../index.md). “No extraction found” means no court-specific extraction was linked there; it does **not** establish that no internal, unpublished, historical or unreviewed classification exists.
- Jurisdiction/subject summaries: GOV.UK first where a relevant page was found; otherwise judiciary.uk. Where the table links to judiciary.uk, no suitable GOV.UK subject-description page was identified for that exact directory entry in this pass. These prose descriptions may help build a vocabulary but are not presumed to be machine-readable court taxonomies.
