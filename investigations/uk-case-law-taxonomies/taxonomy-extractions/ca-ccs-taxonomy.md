# Citizens Advice CCS labels (CA_CCS)

Source file: `docs/CA_CCS_taxonomy_narrow.csv` (user-provided). Result of a research project by Caitlin Wilson, based on the organisation of the [Citizens Advice website](https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/) — CA page labels mapped into CA top-level areas. 

Many thanks to Caitlin for providing the full scheme from which this simplified view is derived.

Structure: 108 labels in 7 areas (two levels). Area names follow the citizensadvice.org.uk section headings. Where a label matches a term in [LAMS CCS v1.6](lams-ccs-taxonomy.md) case-insensitively, the CCS term is shown after an em dash, more details LAMS mappings have been developed but are not reported here.
