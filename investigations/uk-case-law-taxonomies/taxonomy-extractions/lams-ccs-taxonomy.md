# LAMS Content Classification Scheme (CCS)

Source file: `docs/CCS_taxonomy_narrow.csv` — CCS v1.6, the content classification scheme of the [Legal and Advice Metadata Scheme (LAMS)](https://plenet.org.uk/classifying-information/). Developed in 2001 by the Department for Constitutional Affairs (the former Lord Chancellor's Department) with the [Advice Services Alliance](https://asauk.org.uk/) as part of LAMS, to power "intelligent search" on the Just Ask website (now [Community Legal Advice](https://www.communitylegaladvice.org.uk/)); it built on earlier schemes from Citizens Advice, Shelter and DIAL UK. The CCS is free to use, was maintained by Advicenow for the Community Legal Advice team at the Legal Services Commission, and governed by a user panel (Advice Services Alliance, Advicenow, Citizens Advice, Shelter). See also the [2000 consultation coverage](https://www.lawgazette.co.uk/news/metadata-scheme-welcome-but-language-fears-voiced/31476.article) and [Binary Law's account of the CLS proposals](https://www.binarylaw.co.uk/2000/12/25/meta-data-proposals-for-legal-advice-sites/).

Structure: 346 terms — 14 top headings (`T`) with `G` preferred terms beneath, two to three levels deep. 16 terms are cross-listed under more than one parent and appear under each. Order within a level follows the source file's narrower-term lists.
