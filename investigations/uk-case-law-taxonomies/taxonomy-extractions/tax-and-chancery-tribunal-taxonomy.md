# Taxonomy extraction: GOV.UK "Tax and Chancery tribunal decisions" — Category list

Full transcription of the flat single-level `Category` facet in the search form at [gov.uk/tax-and-chancery-tribunal-decisions](https://www.gov.uk/tax-and-chancery-tribunal-decisions) (GOV.UK finder, "From: HM Courts & Tribunals Service and Upper Tribunal (Tax and Chancery Chamber)"; decisions on tax, financial services, pensions, charity and land registration appeals to the UT (TCC); 1,461 results at capture). Context for the chamber's legacy subject handling is in [courts-tribunals-schemes.md](../notes/courts-tribunals-schemes.md) §5, which recorded that the older TCC decisions site had *no* subject classification — this GOV.UK finder is the first browsable category list observed for the chamber.

- Banking
- Charity
- Financial Services
- Land Registration
- Pensions
- Tax

Extraction method: read from the server-rendered `Category` checkbox group in the static HTML (captured 4 Nov 2026). The only other filter is release date; there is no sub-category level.
