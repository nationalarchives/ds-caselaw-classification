# Taxonomy extractions

Transcriptions and structured extractions of existing classification schemes, collected for the [UK case law subject taxonomies](../index.md) investigation.

- [lands-chamber-taxonomy](lands-chamber-taxonomy.md) — Upper Tribunal (Lands Chamber) decisions site: 17 categories / 140 subcategories, from the live `PopulateSubcategory.js` data (site frozen Aug 2025).
- [eat-taxonomy](eat-taxonomy.md) — Employment Appeal Tribunal: flat 43-topic list including 8 explicitly marked obsolete topics (site frozen Mar 2017).
- [eat-taxonomy-govuk](eat-taxonomy-govuk.md) — EAT decisions on GOV.UK: **two-level** scheme, 45 categories / 192 sub-categories parsed from the content API facet schema (captured 13 Nov 2026); includes a comparison with the legacy flat list.
- [land-registration-division-taxonomy](land-registration-division-taxonomy.md) — FTT (Property Chamber) Land Registration Division: **two-level** scheme, 27 categories harvested with their dependent Sub Category options via form postback (208 subcategory entries; site frozen Aug 2025).
- [Sargeant 2026 case law classification](Sargeant%202026%20case%20law%20classification.md)
- [stanford-list-taxonomy](stanford-list-taxonomy.md) — Stanford LIST (Legal Issues Taxonomy): full 1,301-term hierarchy extracted from [taxonomy.legal](https://www.taxonomy.legal/) /api/terms, 20 top-level domains, 4 levels deep.
- [thesaurus-current](thesaurus-current.md) — the national archives' draft legislation thesaurus (CSV in `docs/thesaurus_current.csv`): 494 terms in 3 partitions (Subject — 37 policy areas, Business — NACE-based, Regions), tree view with AltLabels in parentheses.
- [lams-ccs-taxonomy](lams-ccs-taxonomy.md) — LAMS Content Classification Scheme v1.6 (CSV in `docs/CCS_taxonomy_narrow.csv`): 346 terms, 14 top headings, 2–3 levels deep; the 2001 advice-sector scheme behind Just Ask / Community Legal Advice search.
- [ca-ccs-taxonomy](ca-ccs-taxonomy.md) — Citizens Advice CCS labels (CSV in `docs/CA_CCS_taxonomy_narrow.csv`): Caitlin Wilson research project; 108 CA page labels mapped into 7 CA website areas, flat; largely complementary to LAMS CCS (only 11 exact term overlaps).
- [uksc-area-of-law-taxonomy](uksc-area-of-law-taxonomy.md) — UK Supreme Court cases page "Area of law" filter: flat 25-item checkbox list extracted from the site's JS bundle (captured 4 Nov 2026); includes two out-of-order appends (*Veterinary surgeons*, *Mission and Pastoral Measures*).
- [upper-tribunal-aat-taxonomy](upper-tribunal-aat-taxonomy.md) — GOV.UK "Administrative appeals tribunal decisions" finder (UT Administrative Appeals Chamber): **two-level** scheme, 63 categories / 486 sub-categories parsed from the server-rendered facet checkboxes and matched by `{category} - ` prefix (captured 4 Nov 2026); benefit-led, with three out-of-order `Transport - …` appends in the category list.
- [tax-and-chancery-tribunal-taxonomy](tax-and-chancery-tribunal-taxonomy.md) — GOV.UK "Tax and Chancery tribunal decisions" finder (UT Tax and Chancery Chamber): flat 6-category checkbox list from the server-rendered facet (captured 4 Nov 2026); first browsable subject list observed for the chamber.
- [gazette-notice-codes-taxonomy](gazette-notice-codes-taxonomy.md) — The Gazette notice codes: **two-level** scheme of 7 groups / 22 categories / 258 numbered notice codes, plus 40 flat Company Law Supplement codes (A1–L2), parsed from the server-rendered page (captured 4 Nov 2026); a notice-type classification rather than a case-law subject taxonomy.
- [sali-lmss-area-of-law-taxonomy](sali-lmss-area-of-law-taxonomy.md) — SALI LMSS ontology (viewer.sali.org): "Area of Law" subtree, 26 areas / 102 subtopics / 3 leaves walked from the public `lmss_v2.json` in the viewer's own collation order (captured 4 Nov 2026); a US-centric legal-matter classification with `CANADA:`-prefixed entries.
- [Legal subject areas](Legal%20subject%20areas.md) — draft list of legal subject areas based on common areas of law from curricula (Blackstone's Statutes and BSB Bar Qualification Manual).
