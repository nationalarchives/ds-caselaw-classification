**Superseded for post-2017 decisions:** the GOV.UK finder publishes decisions from December 2017 onwards with a two-level Category/Sub-category scheme — extracted and compared with this list in [eat-taxonomy-govuk](eat-taxonomy-govuk.md).

Note that the two level scheme was present on the old site and the agent-assisted extraction had missed this because of the structure of the site.
