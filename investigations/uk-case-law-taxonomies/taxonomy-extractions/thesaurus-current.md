Source: [Draft legislation thesaurus taxonomy viewer](https://epimorphics.github.io/taxonomy-view-generator/) (data: `concepts.json`, retrieved Nov 2026). Subject partitions cover policy areas legislation relates to; the Business partition is a NACE-based sector scheme (NACE codes from the terms' exact-match links); the Regions partition covers geographical regions a piece of legislation may concern (complements, not replaces, extent/application metadata).
