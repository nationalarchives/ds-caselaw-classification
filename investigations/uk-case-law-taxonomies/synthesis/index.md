# Synthesis

Synthesis pages drawing together the [notes](../notes/index.md) and [taxonomy extractions](../taxonomy-extractions/index.md) into candidate schemes and crosswalks, for the [UK case law subject taxonomies](../index.md) investigation.

- [Candidate public-facing top-level taxonomy](candidate-public-top-level-taxonomy.md) — 17 problem-led headings synthesised primarily from Sargeant, Stanford LIST, LAMS CCS, CA CCS and ICLR, with a doctrine crosswalk and design notes.
- [Legislation thesaurus comparison](legislation-thesaurus-comparison.md) — comparison with the draft legislation Subject partition and recommendation for a unified, legislation-led scheme.
- [Candidate public-facing top-level taxonomy (v2, legislation-aligned)](candidate-public-top-level-taxonomy-v2.md) — 28 problem-led headings rebuilt against the updated thesaurus, each naming its corresponding legislation Subject area(s), with documented omissions and a validation plan.
- [Mapping v2 headings to example case-law schemes](v2-heading-scheme-mapping.md) — hub page for per-scheme crosswalks of the v2 headings, with one page and Sankey diagram per scheme:
  - [Sargeant et al. (level 2)](v2-sargeant-mapping.md)
  - [ICLR Legal Topics](v2-iclr-mapping.md)
  - [ICLR catchwords](v2-iclr-catchwords-mapping.md)
  - [UKSC](v2-uksc-mapping.md)
  - [Legal subject areas (curricula)](v2-legal-subject-areas-mapping.md)
  - [Smart survey search topics](v2-smart-survey-topics-mapping.md) — 172 real user search topics mapped (21 excluded as unmappable and listed), the only scheme that tests the headings against actual public vocabulary.
